During my first year in university, we were tasked with a group project in Python class; pick any topic we'd like and build it using Python. For reference, that was also the time when my obsession with the game 'Darkest Dungeon' had started, therefore I convinced my friends to make a *similar* game, but different story.

<br/>
So, our first idea was:

*"How about we represent the average uni student slang by making the main heroes a bunch of students battling with the game enemies -- the actual uni classes. Let's have them go to the club and play lottery for destress, that would be funny."*

<br/>

Guess our professor's reaction.

<button class="sound-btn" data-sound="slap-oh.mp3">He hated it LMAO.</button> Thought it was an attack on education and our university... which was rather funny.


<br/>
We ended up changing the story to:

*"Let's make it a fantasy environment. The main heroes represent students whose fear for exams turn university classes into monsters; so let's have them 'combat' with their fears, to promote a possitive message about getting over your fears."*

<br/>

Aaand just like that, this idea went through, EVEN THOUGH the professor still was not *that* excited. But we got to work!


## 🪸 What the game features.
- Original character art. The backgrounds were taken from 'Darkest Dungeon'
- Turn based combat and 4 playable characters.
- 3 levels with 3 bosses.
- The stress mechanic. Enemies strike both to body and mind.
