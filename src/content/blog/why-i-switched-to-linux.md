While Windows is the regular/ popular choice which works for everything and every app, what made me switch over to the penguin you might ask? Here's my story.

Around a year and a half ago, I started being more aware about my privacy online and searched for ways to keep myself *a bit more private* -- using a VPN to be protected by trackers, switching from gmail over to proton (or simply switching into the proton enviroment) and changing my browser/ search engine.

During that time, the AI epidemic had taken it's toll and started crawling into every app
